# Bhaskar Ghosh | Academic and AI Portfolio

A modern portfolio website showcasing my work in artificial intelligence, machine learning, cybersecurity, computer vision, human-computer interaction, and computing education.

## About

I am an Assistant Professor of Computer Science and Cybersecurity at St. Cloud State University. My work connects academic research, applied software development, student mentorship, and community-focused technology.

This portfolio highlights selected research projects, publications, teaching experience, awards, technical skills, outreach activities, and professional accomplishments.

## Featured Projects

### CharmGuard

A Rust-based intelligent firewall designed to detect behavioral drift and anomalous activity in critical systems using machine learning.

### ElderConnect

A human-centered AI initiative that helps older adults recognize and prevent phishing, online scams, and other digital threats.

### PyroScan

A UAV-based wildfire behavior prediction and direction-monitoring system developed through undergraduate research and published through IEEE.

### Fake Profile Detection

Research exploring generative models, anomaly detection, and modern AI techniques for identifying deceptive social media accounts.

### AdviSync

A student-focused advising platform designed to improve communication and coordination between academic advisors and students.
